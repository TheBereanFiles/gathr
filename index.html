You don't need to be an expert. Jesus promised to be with even two or three (Matt 18:20). Your job is to open your home, open the Word, and let everyone take part (1 Cor 14:26). Here's a simple flow for about 2 hours.

The flow of a gathering

Discovery Bible Study

A simple method anyone can lead. Nobody lectures. The group discovers together, the Holy Spirit teaches, and Scripture is the authority.

Host tips

• Ask, don't tell. Your job is to ask questions.
• Let silence breathe. People need a moment to think.
• Steer back to the text: "Where do we see that in the passage?"
• Share the work: rotate who leads, hosts and brings food.
• Multiply: when you have 12-15 people, raise up a new host.
